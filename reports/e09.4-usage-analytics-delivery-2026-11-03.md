## Summary

Implemented the **E09.4 Usage Analytics** layer: an administrative API over durable admissions (`resource_admissions`), quota utilization (E09.2), and concurrency capacity (E09.3). Dashboards are out of scope.

## Endpoints

| Method | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GET | `/api/resource-analytics/admissions` | Total and daily UTC series (gap-filled); optional `provider_id` and `model_id` filters |
| GET | `/api/resource-analytics/admissions/distribution` | Daily breakdown by provider and provider/model |
| GET | `/api/resource-analytics/quota-utilization` | Quota state and `utilization_percent` (0–100) |
| GET | `/api/resource-analytics/concurrency` | Snapshot of active and available slots |

Range parameters: `from` and `to` (RFC3339 or `YYYY-MM-DD`), inclusive, maximum 366 UTC days.

## Technical decisions

- **Volume source of truth:** `resource_admissions` with `allowed = true`; no second ledger.
- **Global total:** distinct admission count; provider/model breakdowns must not be summed into the global total.
- **Time series:** days without records return `admitted_count: 0`.
- **Disabled quota:** `remaining_requests = 0`, `utilization_percent = 0` (aligned with the E09.2 handler).
- **Truncated SPEC:** complete disabled-policy rules are documented in `E09.4-usage-analytics.md` as assumptions pending lead confirmation.
